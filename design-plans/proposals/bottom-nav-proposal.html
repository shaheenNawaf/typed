Bottom Navigation — Redesign

Principle: everything a one-handed user needs lives inside the thumb's arc — capture, the four core destinations, and the sections currently stranded behind a top-left hamburger.

00Audit of the current bar

HIGHSecondary sections unreachable one-handed

Archive, Trash, Meetings, Journal, Pinned and Today exist only in the drawer, which opens from a hamburger in the top-left corner — the worst reach on a tall phone.

HIGHNo capture affordance

Typed is capture-first, but the bar has no create action. Capture lives a step away (Home → New note, or the Notes FAB), not in the thumb's resting zone.

MEDIUMActive state is a color swap

Icon + label change color instantly — no sliding indicator, no motion. Screen readers also get no selected state.

MEDIUMNo haptics, no badge surface

Tab switches are silent (no selection haptic) and the bar can't surface counts like open tasks.

ARefined classic — same 4 tabs, modern affordances
