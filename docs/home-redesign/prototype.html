What changed

  • Next up → Today. The overdue bill comes first instead of whatever list was edited last. The date moves out of the task text ("— Fri, Nov 6, 2 PM") into a chip or time column. Later becomes Tomorrow, which really moves it.
  • The rest of today shows under it, grouped into Any time, Morning, Afternoon and Evening (like Apple Reminders), with reminder times from voice memos.
  • "₱4,660 spent in November" → Until payday. What's left until the 15th, a daily amount, and a marker for where you should be today. It was also below the fold; now it's on the first screen.
  • Pick up where you left off → Recent: three items instead of one, including voice memos.
  • The "Swipe up for more" pill is gone, since it covered content.
  • From your notes, the capture bar, the header and the dock are unchanged.

3 · On your home screen

Samsung's Now Brief puts a daily summary on the home screen, but the Galaxy S23 doesn't officially get it. A Typed widget can do that job with the same blocks as Home: what's next today and money until payday. It combines today's separate To-do and Finance widgets into one.

The widget

  • Tick tasks without opening the app. The existing To-do widget already queues ticks for when Typed opens.
  • Same order as Home: overdue first, then by time.
  • Money until payday sits at the bottom, the same number as the Home card.
  • Mic and + start a voice memo or a new note directly.

Built on patterns from apps that already do this well: Todoist and Things 3 (one Today list, overdue first); Kaban, a Philippine budgeting app (money "safe to spend until sweldo, about ₱558 a day"); Microsoft To Do (offering undated tasks on a quiet day); Apple Reminders (Today grouped by time of day); Structured (moving unfinished tasks to a new day); Monarch Money (a weekly recap card you can hide); Samsung Now Brief (a daily summary on the home screen). Needs one new setting: payday (default the 15th and 30th) and an optional budget per cutoff. Without a budget, the card shows "₱4,660 spent since payday".
